*The world of M&M is divided into three respective continents though there is technically four, the fourth one is less developed and has the possibility of being more developed and modified later one for it to not be recognizable. For the ease of my own brain, I focused on a plan to flesh out the three continents before me over adding more but the fourth continent will be developed as soon as I deem the other three finished or serviceable enough. Anyways, back to continents, [[4- Magic & Mayhem - Continents/Tholoth\|Tholoth]] is where M&M's so called 'Plot' starts. [[4- Magic & Mayhem - Continents/Tholoth\|Tholoth]], [[4- Magic & Mayhem - Continents/Roylar\|Roylar]], and [[4- Magic & Mayhem - Continents/Arkadios\|Arkadios]] act as the basis of this world's politics and main quests, these are the intended places for our games to be set, it doesn't mean stories set elsewhere is discouraged, just know that's the reason for our focus on them.*
