Terra Occasus, informally known as The Land of the Setting Sun, is a continent with not much known about it aside from certain races and the ever elusive producer of silk known as the Great Celestial State of Wei. The tales that arrive from the traders who manage to cross the Great Divide, depict a continent different than Roylar yet similar in other ways, tales of lush fields and mountains to the grand steppe which dominates most of the land that connects the continent to the Great divide. Culturally, aside from tales of the Oni, Kev, and Wei itself, there is little other sources to go on and many who have been to Wei are mostly traders or adventures who don't stock up on history books.

Connected to Roylar in the way of the Great Barrier, the one actual route connecting the two continents lies in the southern part of both continents with the Nadirans on the east and the Oni in the west, the waters around the Great Barrier are known to be very treacherous, from sea creatures to rapid winds and waves to constant storms, this mix of conditions has rendered sea travel between the continents practically impossible.

Politically we know that the continent is mostly united under the banner of the Wei dynasty with the Azure Throne acting as the symbol of power across the land. The Oni hold control of the pass that eventually connects to Roylar but are divided by several warlords who all bow to the Azure Throne in the form of giving tribute and keeping the trading pass clear of their feuds. The Kev once held most of the grand steppe as their homeland but due to continuous efforts to civilize it by the Wei they have since been forced either to submit to the dynasty or flee eastwards Roylar which has been occurring for close to two thousand years.

Though intercontinental travel has become more common than before, Terra Occasus is still a mystery to be solved. The day, Roylar manages to bridge the Great Barrier or the Wei value their trade so much to conquer the pass in full, will be the day this mystery would be solved.
