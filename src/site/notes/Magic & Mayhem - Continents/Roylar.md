*The Continent of Roylar, originally named Tharn, is also a continent of numerous biomes but less than [[Magic & Mayhem - Continents/Tholoth\|Tholoth]] and more than [[Magic & Mayhem - Continents/Arkadios\|Arkadios]], once filled with numerous magical beasts from Dragons to Giants, it has since lost its magical illusiveness and became a more mundane continent. Like [[Magic & Mayhem - Continents/Arkadios\|Arkadios]], it is not fully explored, the Great Divide, a mountain range said to be the size of whole kingdoms, has split Roylar from its western neighbor and continent called the Terra Occasus {Land of the Setting Sun} by the inhabitants of Roylar, travel between the two is very rare and happens entirely in the southeastern part of the Great Divide where Nadira holds the only land route between the two continents.*

*Roylar is a continent divided into many regions unlike [[Magic & Mayhem - Continents/Tholoth\|Tholoth]], whose regions are named after clans, or [[Magic & Mayhem - Continents/Arkadios\|Arkadios]] who has one region explored. West, Central, North and Eastern Roylar are quite different from each other with numerous cultures in each region and each one having their one name for such regions. Southern Roylar is entirely called Nadira due to the dominance of said culture and state in the region. Roylar's cultures originally diverse with many clans, tribes and communities has since been transformed into more unified polities, once by the forced hands of the Tholothii, then by the centralization policies of Rex Roylar and his descendants, and now by the natural flow of society as it solidifies into the new age. Some cultures, still retain their ancient ties to tribal society like that of the Valdians or the Courish, while others under the Empire of Roylar, are either enticed or forced to abandon their old traditions in favor of a unified identity.*

*Roylar is divided politically and unlike [[Magic & Mayhem - Continents/Tholoth\|Tholoth]], it is not united by one state though by technicality it was once in the olden days. By 1982 PR, The Empire of Roylar or as it is called sometimes, the Roylar Empire or the fifth Roylar empire or The Ventolar Empire, or finally third Ventolar empire with its numerous names and identities depending on the state it interacts with, holds the center of the continent under its velvet boot with its only other polity being the Kingdom of Alfaine which was split into three dutchies by the empire after their most recent war. East Roylar holds the many successor states of the Grand Kingdom of Fiuma which once controlled most of the eastern coast, it also houses the Empire of Valdia, The Vistula Cantons and Divided Courland. West Roylar holds The Korinsky Resardom and the Badlands. North Roylar holds the Vendri tribes. South Roylar or Nadira, holds the Caliphate of Nadira which quite like Tholoth even though it is depicted united by outsiders, the ones who live in Nadira know it as a divided land with emirs, sultans and even religious orders taking control of whole regions and acting as their rulers rather than the Caliph. Unlike Tholoth, it is not a closed state and is the largest trading hub of the continent with Central Roylar being the second largest.*

*One thing to not forget is that numerous islands and archipelagos of the continent have been seized by the Nelaeryn Western Expedition with them also holding quite the influence on the successor states of Fiuma, they act both as ruthless settlers and pirates who occasionally raid the continent to keep their coffers afloat or to keep the continent on edge.*
