Carden is a goddess of life, and her religion existed long before Roylar, before Illiothien. She is seen as a benevolent goddess who forgives sinners who repent and keeps her heart open to all her followers. She values all life and sees the preservation and protection of life as one of the most important things a person can do. The Church's doctrine is enforced across its lands, and the faith remained unchanged until recently, when a new schism arose, dividing the faithful in two and risking an inter-faith war that could cost thousands of lives.

The faith is led by a Head Priestess, who must be a woman. She is able to gain the power called "The Psyche of Godhood," which is bestowed randomly upon a child a decade before the death of a Head Priestess, who will get a dream prophesizing their death. The child must be found by the church. Mages have not been able to study the power, doing so would be heresy, but it is known to grant telepathic abilities and the ability to see the future through dreams. All priestesses have been human since the dawn of history, though they come from different parts of the continent, with culture having no influence on their selection. The faith does not allow homosexuality and sees it as a sin not to create life. Priests and Priestesses are encouraged to marry, except for the Head Priestess, who becomes infertile upon receiving her power. As such, her position is also seen as a sacrifice by believers.

Carden is always depicted as a human woman, though ancient statues of unknown age in the easternmost cities of the continent once showed her as an elvish woman, when the species of man, who had never saw elves, thought it looked more divine. Since then, those statues have been modified to remove the pointed ears an elf would have. She is always depicted as beautiful, but her expression changes from region to region: the east shows her crying, while the west shows her smiling. The Courland is split on these representations, so you will see a different one at each church. Her symbol is the Tree of Life, said to be her home, with its peak reaching the stars. It is usually green, though variations in red have appeared with the schism. The church's home is in Leoncour, on a small island in the north owned by the church and guarded by the Argevin Guard, who haven't left the island since. It was moved from Mezzota after the fall of Fiuma, as it was seen as dangerous to keep the priestess there due to rumors of another elvish invasion.

Carden sees all races as equal, but views elves as misguided individuals who need to be saved from their overlord, Tholoth. It is from this doctrine that the schism began. A priest in year 1950PR in the lands of Mezzota named Martino de Diavolo walked into his church and cried out to the people about the injustice his people, all people, had suffered at the hands of the elves. They were not misguided, he said, but evil by nature, enemies to all faithful of Carden, and they needed to be vanquished so the faithful could live in the peace Carden preaches. The church made no comment on his views, except to take away his right to preach. That only emboldened his followers and led to the schism now threatening the balance of the faith. Day by day, the eastern lands lean more toward his views, and the Head Priestess has said nothing,  neither prosecuting him for his beliefs nor supporting him.
