Tholoth's Truth once was known as the Pantheon of Zeovrulen, consists of numerous gods who once had their own religious orders, back when the whole elvish continent was divided into groups of varying faiths. When Illiothien united the continent and established his "Eternal" empire, one of the first things he promoted was the idea of a unified faith, so that his empire wouldn't suffer religious turmoil while conquering the world. And so Tholoth's Truth was made. Most elvish gods were included, but all under Tholoth, he was considered the founding god of all, and every other god was related to him in some way.

Tholoth is considered the ruler of the gods and their creator, though many of his descendant gods are not products of his marriage to Derivia (who, like him, has no creator). Tholoth, Derivia, and Candia all came from the Gates of Heaven, and all three are considered the first gods, made entirely out of the essence of the world. Tholoth created everything and made the elves in his image, while his wife Derivia made the dark elves in hers. Candia busied herself creating the numerous wild beasts that roam the world. After the destruction of a species lost to history, Tholoth declared that elves would be the only species of the world.

Candia defied her brother by making a new species (theorized to be the humans) and sailing with her creations across the seas to escape her brother's wrath. Such an event was seen as a sign of weakness, and it led to the War of the Gods, which is said to have caused unfathomable amounts of suffering and death. Tholoth was then weakened but still used his last bit of strength to defeat the other gods, sending them to a life of purgatory where their cries would never be heard. And so these gods eventually became demons. Heaven, meanwhile, is where Tholoth resides behind the Gates of Heclia, nursing his wounds, and where all good souls go after death.

The Faith is led by the Imperial Ecclesiarchy, which is governed by a council of High Lords educated in all matters of inter-elvish faith. They dictate the traditions and festivals of each region respectively, with only some areas having autonomous rights to conduct such things, granted by the council. This is a situation the Ecclesiarchy doesn't approve of, and day by day they increase their hold on the religious affairs of all who live under the empire. The Ecclesiarchy is also the one who protects the Gates of Heclia and ensures that the gates remain worthy of Tholoth's gaze.

The Ecclesiarchy deems all species lesser than elves. This became more engraved into the faith with Illiothien's codification of Tholoth's Truth, which put to law that all species other than elves would be enslaved or destroyed, with half-elves needing to prove themselves worthy of Tholoth's embrace.

#### *The list of the gods and their relation in hierarchical order*

- *Tholoth (Creation)*
- *Derivia (Death) — considered a dead goddess after the death of her species/followers*
- *Candia (Love) — considered a heretical goddess*
- *Yeraleth (Time) — Tholoth's son, born from Derivia*
- *Toralia (Storm) — Tholoth's daughter, born from Derivia*
- *Melazshara (War and Conquest) — Tholoth's daughter and Ateron's twin, not born from Derivia*
- *Ateron (Smithing and Metals) — Tholoth's son and Melazshara's twin, not born from Derivia*
- *Relion (Music and Dance) — son of Yeraleth and Ulieria*
- *Kalina (Harvest and Agriculture) — daughter of Toralia and Jercon*
- *Jercon (Wine) — created by Tholoth out of essence, but not considered blood*
- *Ulieria (Lust) — created by Tholoth out of essence, but not considered blood*
