The Faith of the Two is the oldest and most important faith of the Weiben people, and in turn of Valdia. Even though smaller religions and sects exist, its importance in the daily life of the simple Valdian citizen cannot be denied. It follows integral church law, and its churches take instruction from the Central Church of the Two, relocated from Rainarch and rebuilt in Silberstadt after the unification.

The church is led by two Popes who represent the will of the Two. Their genders are not fixed. Their duties consist of daily prayer and protecting the interests of the faithful, and as such the church holds considerable influence in the country. Its history has been mostly clean, though there have been instances where one pope went against the other's verdict, or supported a person or nation pursuing war. The faith isn't exactly a doctrine of pacifism, but it still promotes the idea of peace across the continent, and the church advocates for peaceful resolutions to all conflicts where possible.

The Two allow homosexuality, though they do not exactly promote it, they see reproduction as a duty of all. (They do not prosecute homosexuals and are officially mandated to love their fellow faithful no matter their sexuality.) They also allow marriage between their priests, but they do not permit forced conversion, and they see other religions as misguided rather than scorned. The Two's priests are taught the basics of all other faiths, and all believers are encouraged to teach their children the basics of all faiths as well, letting them choose their own when they become adults. Valdia, Wielkonia, and Vistula are mostly filled with followers of the Two, though it is not rare to see believers of forgotten or foreign faiths.

The Two are considered guardian angels who protect all under the sky. Their paradise is open to all, no matter their faith, as is their hell, which is being denied entry into heaven and suffering purgatory until the end, where they will relive their worst moments until the end of the world. Their church takes donations, but the faithful are not required to pay, though that doesn't stop the governments from sending a sizable donation every new year. The faith's symbol is two suns side by side, colored dark teal or black, though the color is not strictly enforced, you can find variations all across the churches.

The Two are idolized as two statues of two people with white wings (for reference, the [[The Faith of Antelon \|Faith of Antelon]]'s angels have a similar wing type). The gender and race of the statues are not determined by the church; that decision is left to the local church, which chooses whichever representation best fits its churchgoers. As such, you can find a church with both female, both male, or mixed statues. The most common representation of the Two, the same one seen in Silberstadt, is two male humans, one looking older and wiser, the other young and wary.

The faith also sees all races as equal, elves included, and as such their lands hold the largest demographic of elves and half-elves outside of Tholoth. Even though the doctrine was briefly forgotten after the Nelaeryn Empire's fall and the beginning of the persecution of elves, the Central Church regained control of its faithful in 300 PR and had started the work of introducing the faithful to a new idea of what an elf can be.
