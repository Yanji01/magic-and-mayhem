Antelon is a god who sees all other divine beings as imitations of himself, except [[3- Magic & Mayhem - Faiths/Tholoth's Truth\|Tholoth]] and the elvish gods, whom he views as devils and demons. Antelon values honor and is regarded as the supreme being, the creator, and the principal object of faith. The Faith can be considered monotheistic, holding that there is only one deity: an all-supreme being universally referred to as God, as such the name Antelon comes from those not of the faith. The Church considers itself the one true faith and all other religions misguided and heretical. It actively prosecutes all who are not of its faith. There is a heaven guarded by Antelon's angels, and a hell with Tholoth as its ruler, where demons and devils reside. By now it has become Church doctrine to eradicate all traces of elves and their culture, no matter the cost in life.

The faith's head is the Patriarch, who resides in Brascow, the capital of the Tsardom of Korinsky. The Patriarch, as Bishop of Brascow, is the head of the Holy See, which is assisted by the various departments of the Antelon Bishoprics. He makes decisions on issues of faith and morality for Antelonians throughout the world. The Patriarch is seen to have full and supreme jurisdiction over the Church in matters of faith and morals, as well as in church discipline and government. All Popes have been male, and all have been human. The Church sees other species as inferior and unfit to live upon God's land. Homosexuality is seen as a sin and made Illegal.

All those who are not of the Antelon faith are captured and burned at the stake. All who defy the Church are burned at the stake. All who try to change the status quo are burned at the stake. The faith sees the burning of its victims as a way to grant absolution for their crimes, using it to execute heretics so as to grant them a second chance in the afterlife. In doing so, other methods of execution are seen as cursed to the hells and are reserved for the worst criminals. The faith's symbol is the wings of an angel of heaven. Their churches hold statues of Antelon himself, with angelic wings and a halo crown. He is depicted with a sword said to be hidden somewhere on the continent, to be found only by a worthy believer who will vanquish the demonic forces of this world.
