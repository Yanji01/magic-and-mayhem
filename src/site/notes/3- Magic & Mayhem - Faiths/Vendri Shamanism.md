The Vendri faith consists of worshipping the gods and revering the ancestors of old. It focuses on what can be achieved in life and what can be left to your descendants. The main gods are Iduun, Fulla, and Foseti, who act as the leaders of all divine beings in the north. Iduun, god of lightning, is king of the gods and resides on Mount Halti, where he and his fellow gods live for eternity. Fulla is the goddess of war and wife of Iduun; she controls the army of gods and demigods who reside on the mountain and gives blessings to all warriors. Foseti is the god of wisdom and an adviser to the couple, and he also keeps his own hold upon the mountain. He values progress and change. Vendri heaven, unlike others, is a place only warriors go to. There they party and duel to their hearts' content, stopping only when the time for the World's End comes. Vendri hell is a cold landscape where the dark spirits of the world torture all who fall into their domain. Any warrior who dies not in battle or by a weapon wound is sent down there, as they are cowards.

The head of the faith is non-existent. Each Jarldom has its own shaman who, unlike the orcs, doesn't answer to any organization except the gods. The Warrior Council acts as something akin to a religious organization, as it is the main body that decides which warrior is honored on the Wall of Legends and which warrior is forgotten. The Wall of Legends is a stone wall the size of a whole building, where the most famous warriors and kings have their names inscribed to be remembered for future generations. Council members are chosen from veteran commanders or warriors whose experience is used as a basis to decide the process. Vendri himself still has the biggest name inscribed upon the wall. It is said that Vendri was originally a man who gained divinity through his beast-fighting and was accepted into Mount Halti as a demigod, where he still resides, his deeds earned him eternal adoration by the northern people who started calling themselves the Vendri to call upon his power.

The faith allows all species except Elves, Half-elves, and Orcs into the fold, and actively hunts for all three in their lands. Even with the population of northern orcs in the area, the doctrine is still in place and is practiced by every Jarl. The symbol of the faith is the one inscribed upon Vendri's own sword, which is kept safe by the Warrior Council. It is said to be given only to the person worthy of Vendri's legacy, and worthy of all the ancestors that have been and will be.
