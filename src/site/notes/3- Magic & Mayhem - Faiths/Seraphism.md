*Seraphism is a polytheistic faith and the dominant religion of the Elgarian Empire, though it takes its name and origin from Vlenna. Its founding claim is that all deities of all faiths are real, and that differing names and interpretations are often the same divine beings seen from different angles, a doctrine known as Multiplicitas Divinatis. The gods are not ranked, and no deity may be dismissed as false, though the faith holds that they also cannot simply be picked and chosen, since mortal understanding of divinity is too limited for such shopping. What the faith distinguishes instead is the Tutator, or patron deity: gods who have bestowed favor upon a particular people or nation. The Elgarian Pantheon was assembled from exactly this principle, blending the deities of the empire's founder species and its neighbors into a single framework, and any other nation that converts to Seraphism is expected to take its own patrons rather than adopt Elgar's.*

*According to the Seraphist scriptures, reality was born from an explosion of energy whose cause is frankly unknown, and the faith is content to say so, its cosmology is a theory rather than a revelation. Behind that explosion stands the Forge of Creation, a realm of infinite energy where universes form the way planets do, itself merely a byproduct of a greater plane, and so on without end. From the first cry of the universe came the Elder Gods, beings born of aspects of reality itself, and only much later the younger deities. Among the Elgarian patrons are Lyra, the star-clad mother who came to the empire through the Lyran Manea and who loved mortals for their passion and longing for the stars; Syntheon, lord of the forge and patron of knowledge and invention, who gave the world's species their spark of thought as a test and was astonished when they surpassed it; Virelia, mother nature and the world itself, angry and ambitious and long disappointed in her children; and Aequitas, the youngest of the pantheon, lady of justice and retribution. The faith's central tenet is Ascension: an adherent should always better themselves, learn new skills, and teach others, while the Sanctity of Flesh forbids self-wounding and the Sanctity of Sapience forbids the sacrifice of intelligent beings.*

*Because the faith holds that all gods exist, Seraphism has no single afterlife, it holds that the soul may enter whichever domain it best aligns with, and that through action and will the faithful may ascend into the ranks of the Seraphs, the faith's revered dead, who protect the living from beyond the veil as Archangels, Orreries, Aranaks, and Cherubs, the last being the souls of children lost before their time. The church grew out of the Time of Chains, when enslaved peoples' faiths were united against the Naelaryn elves, and remained a minor cult valued for its compassion well into the Elgarian Golden Age before spreading through the later Black Sun Xenocides to become the empire's main faith. It is governed without a central figure, through councils that mirror the Elgarian state, and holds no political power by design, a secular arrangement the church itself engineered to prevent future conflict. Its holy orders, chiefly the Knights Sacramento and the Ordo Arbiter, carry the faith most visibly: militantly tolerant, apolitical in principle, and sworn to war only against daemonic cults or threats to the world entire.*
