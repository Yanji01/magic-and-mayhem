The Faiths of the Magic and Mayhem are mixed with original ideas and ones inspired by real life. M&M is intentionally made in a way, where the existence of the gods are never confirmed, it is both to differentiate it from other settings with walking or interfering gods but to also create the mystery of if they exist. That aside, M&M in reality has numerous religions and open space for such religions to be created by others but there are a few major ones entirely made for the setting and usually play a part in events of the world and are a significant player on the board that is M&M.
